Our story

Family recipes. Hayward roots.

At our first location on A Street, the owner lived near the restaurant for years, watching as they served Indian food and dreaming of opening an Afghan restaurant in the neighborhood. In 2014 the Indian restaurant moved and Ghazni Afghan Kabobs was born. Our food is the product of a lifelong dream of sharing our family recipes from Afghanistan with people who want to try new cuisines.

Our Winton location opened its doors in May of 2019, and we are committed to keep going forward with our mission of serving high quality food, friendly service and a clean atmosphere for our guests and community.
